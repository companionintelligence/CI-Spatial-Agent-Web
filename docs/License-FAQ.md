# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what CI Spatial Companion's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute CI Spatial Companion source code for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

CI Spatial Companion is the Companion Intelligence WebXR/WebSpatial 3D avatar companion client.

CI Spatial Companion uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

### What freedoms do you have as a CI Spatial Companion user?

If you use CI Spatial Companion for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork CI Spatial Companion?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute CI Spatial Companion under the same license.

### Can I contribute to CI Spatial Companion?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell CI Spatial Companion?

No. Selling CI Spatial Companion, including paid access to a hosted instance, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to the rest of Companion Intelligence?

No. Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product. Most other Companion Intelligence product repositories use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories — see the org's open-source rollout tracker for the current list.
